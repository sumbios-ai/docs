description: "The side menu is your way around O."

Start on Home, explore your relationships in Insights and Network, or open Notes to keep something you want to remember. Other pages appear according to your plan and access.

## Where to find it

Use the left application sidebar. The menu can collapse; on mobile, navigation is presented in a compact layout.

## What you can do

- Select a label to open that destination.
- Collapse the side menu when you want more room for the page.
- On mobile, open the navigation menu; Field Mode is also offered in mobile navigation.

## How to use it

- Choose Insights for a summary of your network and relationships you may want to follow up on.
- If a destination is absent, check your account access and workspace preferences before assuming it is unavailable.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** The pages you see depend on your plan, your navigation preferences, and your screen size. This guide covers the everyday application, not Admin.
</Note>
