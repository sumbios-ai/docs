Start on Home, look at your relationships in Insights and Network, or open Notes to keep something you want to remember. Which other pages you see depends on your plan and what you have access to.

## Where to find it

The menu runs down the left side of the app. You can collapse it. On mobile, it is shown in a smaller layout.

<Frame caption="The side menu">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/sidebar.png" alt="The side menu: Home, Insights, Network, Circles, Notes, Discovery and Notifications, with Referrals & Credits near the bottom" width="200" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Select an item in the menu to open that page.
- Collapse the side menu when you want more room for the page.
- On mobile, open the menu. Field Mode is also in the mobile menu.

## How to use it

- Choose Insights for a summary of your network and the relationships you may want to follow up on.
- If a page is missing from the menu, it may still be available to you. Check what your account has access to, and your workspace preferences.

<Note>
**Good to know.** The pages you see depend on your plan, your menu preferences and your screen size. This guide covers the everyday app, not Admin.
</Note>
