Sumbios O is an executive assistant for your whole network. It remembers the people you know, what you know about them and how you are connected, so you can ask about your network instead of searching through it. O prepares you; you decide what to do.

## When you would use it

Whenever a person matters to what you are doing next: a meeting, an introduction, a hire, a deal, a funding round. You ask O who is involved, what has changed and who can help, and you walk in prepared.

## Your first hour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring your network in">
    Install the O browser extension and import your LinkedIn connections. In Settings → [Connections](/settings/connections), connect Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or WhatsApp. See [Integrations](/reference/integrations) for what each one brings in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell O what you are working on">
    In Settings → [Goals](/settings/goals), choose what you want help with and describe what you are focused on right now. O uses this to shape its answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    On **Home**, ask about a real meeting or a person you need to reach. Not sure what to ask? Start from [the Ask O menu](/use-cases/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what you know">
    Write a note about someone you met this week, or use **Add context** on [Network Health](/insights/network-health). On your phone, [Field Mode](/getting-started/field-mode) lets you speak or type quick notes and save them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with someone you trust">
    Add a co-founder or a close colleague as a [friend](/network-and-circles/friends-and-sharing), and choose what each of you can see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What O does behind it

- **It remembers what you connect.** O works only with the people, accounts and notes you bring in. The more it knows, the better it can help.
- **It prepares; you decide.** O answers in the app. It sends an email or a WhatsApp message, or books a meeting, only when you ask and then confirm. It never posts anything.
- **You control sharing.** Nothing about your network is shared with a friend until you turn it on, one area at a time.
- **It can be wrong.** Check important facts before you rely on them.

<Note>
**Good to know.** What you see depends on your plan, what you have access to and the accounts you have connected. Some pages and features are not on every plan.
</Note>
