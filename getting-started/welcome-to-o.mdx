Start with a question on Home, then explore your members, notes, and other available pages from the side menu.

## Where to find it

Open Help from the sidebar whenever you want to learn a part of O. Return to Home to begin using the application.

## What you can do

- Use Home to ask O a question about a goal or upcoming conversation.
- Explore Network to find a member you know and review their available profile.
- Write a note to keep a detail you want to remember.
- Search Help when you need instructions for a particular page or action.

## How to use it

- Start with a goal you can describe in one sentence.
- Ask O to help you explore options, then check any suggested facts against their sources.
- You choose what to save, share, or do next. Reading Help does not contact anyone.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Not every section is available to every account. Availability depends on your plan, access, connections, and the data you have chosen to bring into O.
</Note>
