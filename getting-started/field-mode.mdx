Field Mode has five tabs: Capture, Search, Chat, Add and Share. Each one is a different way to start.

## Where to find it

Open Field Mode from the side menu on your phone.

## What you can do

- Save a detail about someone you have met, or about an event you are at.
- Search for a person.
- Open the chat to get help while you are out.
- Use the Add tab to add information, or the Share tab to share it.

## How to use it

- Pick the tab that fits what you want to do.
- Before you save, check who the note is about and add the details you want to remember.
- Before you leave the page, check that the details you saved are correct.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Some tabs and actions only work when you are signed in, online and have access. Only save information you have a legitimate reason to keep. Anything shared still follows the sharing settings shown on screen.
</Note>
