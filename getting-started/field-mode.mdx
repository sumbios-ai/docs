Capture, Search, Chat, Add, and Share give you different ways to begin.

## Where to find it

Open Field Mode from the mobile sidebar or visit /field.

## What you can do

- Keep a detail about someone you have met or an event you are attending.
- Search for a person.
- Open chat for help in the field.
- Add information or share using the corresponding mode.

## How to use it

- Choose the mode that matches the task from the Field Mode navigation.
- Check who the information is about and add the details you want to remember before saving.
- Review captured details for accuracy before leaving the page.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Some modes or actions depend on sign-in, connectivity, and access. Only capture information you have a legitimate reason to record; shared information remains subject to the controls shown.
</Note>
