The steps can be different if you joined through an organization or an invitation.

## Where to find it

Use the sign-in or invitation link you were given for your account. The first time you sign in, follow the setup steps on screen before you open Home.

<Frame caption="The sign-in page">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/sign-in.png" alt="Sign in to O: Continue with Google, or email and password" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Go through the setup steps on screen and choose the goals that match your interests.
- Read any request for permission before you connect an account or bring in your network.
- Go back to Settings later to change your goals, your connected accounts or the page you want O to open on.

## How to use it

- Use the account and invitation link meant for you.
- Read each step before you choose Continue. Only give O access to an account you control.
- If your access has ended or a link does not work, use the contact or support option shown on screen. Do not create a second account.

<Note>
**Access and privacy.** Whether you can get in depends on your account status, whether you qualify for an invitation, and any rules set by your organization.
</Note>
