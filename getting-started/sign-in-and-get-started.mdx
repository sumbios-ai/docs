The steps can differ if you joined through an organization or an invitation.

## Where to find it

Use the sign-in or invitation link provided for your account. On first access, follow the on-screen onboarding steps before opening Home.

## What you can do

- Continue through the displayed onboarding steps and choose goals that reflect your interests.
- Review any permission request before connecting an account or importing network data.
- Return to Settings later to change goals, connections, or your preferred start page.

## How to use it

- Use the account and invitation link intended for you.
- Read each step before choosing Continue; only authorize a source you control.
- If access has ended or a link does not work, use the contact or support path shown on the screen rather than creating a duplicate account.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Access depends on account status, invitation eligibility, and any organization policy. O does not receive your external account password through Help.
</Note>
