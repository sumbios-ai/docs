Check the details shown in the chat to see whether O is using the page or an item you have selected as background.

## Where to find it

Choose the Assistant button in the top bar when it is available. For a full conversation and your chat history, open Home.

<Frame caption="The Assistant, pinned beside the page">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/assistant-anywhere.png" alt="The Assistant panel docked at the side of the app" width="420" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Ask a question or describe something you would like help with.
- Continue the current conversation with a follow-up.
- Open Home to find earlier conversations and explore any lists or documents O has created for you.

## How to use it

- Check which page or item the chat is referring to before asking about it.
- Tell O who or what you mean if that is not clear from the page.
- If O has misunderstood, explain what needs to change. Check important details rather than treating every suggestion as a fact.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** O can only use information your account is allowed to access. The chat button and the background information it uses can differ from page to page.
</Note>
