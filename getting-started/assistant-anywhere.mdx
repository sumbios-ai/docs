title: "Open the Assistant from anywhere"
description: "Ask O for help without leaving the page you are on."
---

To see whether O is using the page you are on, or something you selected, as background, look at the details shown in the chat.

## Where to find it

Choose the Assistant button in the top bar, on pages that have it. For a full conversation and your past chats, open Home.

<Frame caption="The Assistant, pinned beside the page">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/assistant-anywhere.png" alt="The Assistant open at the side of the app" width="420" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Ask a question or describe something you would like help with.
- Continue the current conversation with a follow-up.
- Open Home to find earlier conversations and look through any lists or documents O has made for you.

## How to use it

- Before you ask about a page or an item, check that the chat is looking at the right one.
- Tell O who or what you mean if that is not clear from the page.
- If O got it wrong, tell it what to change. Check important details: not every suggestion is a fact.

<Note>
**Good to know.** O can only use information your account is allowed to see. The chat button, and the background information O uses, can change from page to page.
</Note>
