The top bar also has shortcuts to the Assistant, your notifications and your account. Workspace tools puts sharing, chat, reminders and notes in one place.

## Where to find it

Search is in the top bar. Use it to look through everything you have access to. Next to it are the Assistant button, the notification bell, your account menu and Workspace tools.

## What you can do

- Search for a name or a specific phrase, then choose a result to open it.
- To search what friends have shared with you, choose which networks to include in Workspace tools.
- Open the bell for new updates, or the account menu for Settings.
- Choose Set reminder or Add note in Workspace tools, when you see them there.

## How to use it

- Type a specific name, organization or topic.
- If a result has a familiar name, check what kind of result it is before you open it.
- If you expected to see something from a friend's network, check which friends you selected and what each one lets you see.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Search only shows things you are allowed to see, in the networks you have chosen. If something is missing, O may not have that information, or it may be in a network you have not selected.
</Note>
