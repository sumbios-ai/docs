Possible Ties suggests pairs of people who may be connected, and tells you why. Key Connectors shows people who are connected to several different groups. Refreshing can take a few minutes.

## Where to find it

From Insights, open Connectors.

## What you can do

- Try Possible Ties or Key Connectors and read why each person is shown.
- Open a profile to check who the person is and what you know about them.
- Think about whether an introduction would help both people, and ask their permission first.

## How to use it

- Before you rely on a suggested connection, check the profiles or the Network page.
- If you ask someone for an introduction, say why and keep the request specific. Make it easy for them to say no.
- A suggested connection doesn't always mean two people know each other. Only say they do when the information confirms it.

<Note>
**Good to know.** O's suggestions are based on the connections recorded in your network. They don't prove that two people know each other, or that they have agreed to an introduction.
</Note>
