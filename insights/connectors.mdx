---

Possible Ties shows suggestions and the reasons behind them. Key Connectors highlights members with connections across different groups. Refreshing can take a few minutes.

## Where to find it

From Insights, open Connectors or visit /intelligence/hidden-bridges.

## What you can do

- Try Possible Ties or Key Connectors and read why each member appears.
- Open a profile to check who the member is and what you know about them.
- Consider whether an introduction would be useful to both people, and ask their permission first.

## How to use it

- Check the profiles or Network page before relying on a suggested connection.
- If you ask someone for an introduction, explain why and keep the request specific. Give them room to decline.
- A suggested connection does not necessarily mean two people know each other. Only say that when the information confirms it.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** O’s suggestions depend on the connections recorded in your network. They are not proof that two members know each other or have agreed to an introduction.
</Note>
