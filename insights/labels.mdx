---
title: "Labels"
description: "Find the labels you have used to organize the people in your network."
---

For each label you can see its category, any notes, and when it was added. Search by person or label to find what you need.

## Where to find it

Open Labels from Insights.

<Frame caption="Insights → Labels">
  <img src="/images/insights/labels.png" alt="The Labels page, before any labels are added" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Search by person, label or category.
- Choose a person to open their profile.
- Remove a label from a person when you no longer need it.

## How to use it

- Pick labels that are specific and easy to understand.
- Before you delete, check that you have the right person and label selected.
- Clear the search and the category filter to see all labels again.

<Note>
**Good to know.** You can't create or rename categories on the Labels page. Labels help you stay organized. They are not confirmed judgments about anyone.
</Note>
