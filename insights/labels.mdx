title: "Labels"
description: "Find the labels you have used to organize members of your network."
---

See their categories, any notes, and when they were added. Search by member or label to find what you need.

## Where to find it

Open Labels from Insights or visit /intelligence/tags.

<Frame caption="Insights → Labels">
  <img src="/images/insights/labels.png" alt="The Labels page, before any labels are added" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Search by member, label, or category.
- Choose a member to open their profile.
- Remove a label from a member when it is no longer useful.

## How to use it

- Choose specific, understandable labels.
- Check which member and label you have selected before deleting.
- Clear the search and category filter to browse all labels again.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** The Labels page does not create or rename categories. Labels are organizing aids, not verified judgments about members.
</Note>
