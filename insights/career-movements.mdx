A confirmed change of role or workplace can give you a thoughtful reason to catch up.

## Where to find it

Open Career Movements from Insights, or visit /intelligence/alumni-movement.

<Frame caption="Insights → Career Movements">
  <img src="/images/insights/career-movements.png" alt="Career Movements: organisations your contacts came from, with current and alumni counts" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Explore changes of role or company and compare the past and current details.
- Use the date or company filters to focus on the changes that interest you.
- Open a profile to verify the change and source.

## How to use it

- Check when the information was updated. A profile can be out of date.
- If the change is confirmed, it can be a reason to reconnect. It does not tell you what the person plans to do next.
- Clear filters to return to the broader result set.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Movement depends on profile data coverage and freshness. Do not assume that no result means no one has moved.
</Note>
