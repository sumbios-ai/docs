A confirmed new role or new workplace can be a good reason to catch up.

## Where to find it

Open Career Movements from Insights.

<Frame caption="Insights → Career Movements">
  <img src="/images/insights/career-movements.png" alt="Career Movements: the organisations your contacts came from, with how many people are there now and how many are alumni" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Look at changes of role or company, and compare the old and new details.
- Filter by date or company to focus on the changes that interest you.
- Open a profile to check the change and where the information came from.

## How to use it

- Check when the information was last updated. A profile can be out of date.
- A confirmed change can be a reason to reconnect. It doesn't tell you what the person plans to do next.
- Clear the filters to go back to the wider list of results.

<Note>
**Good to know.** What you see here depends on how many profiles O has and how up to date they are. If nothing shows up, don't assume no one has moved.
</Note>
