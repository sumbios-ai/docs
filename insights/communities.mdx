Open a group to see who is in it and the connections O has found between them.

## Where to find it

Open Communities from Insights or visit /intelligence/cluster-insights.

<Frame caption="Insights → Communities">
  <img src="/images/insights/communities.png" alt="Communities shown as bubbles, grouped by domain and industry" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a group to explore its members and what connects them.
- Open a member’s profile to learn more and check the sources.
- Use filters and network choices to focus on the part of your network you want to explore.

## How to use it

- Think of these groups as a way to explore, not an official club or a label people have chosen for themselves.
- Check a member’s details before describing them as part of a community.
- Check which networks are included so you know where the connections come from.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** O finds these groups from the information available in your network. People may describe their own communities differently.
</Note>
