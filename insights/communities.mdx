Open a group to see who is in it and the connections O has found between them.

## Where to find it

Open Communities from Insights.

<Frame caption="Insights → Communities">
  <img src="/images/insights/communities.png" alt="Communities shown as bubbles, grouped by domain and industry" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a group to see the people in it and what connects them.
- Open a person's profile to learn more and check where the information came from.
- Use the filters, and choose which networks to include, to focus on the part of your network you want to look at.

## How to use it

- These groups are a way to explore your network. They aren't official clubs, or labels people have chosen for themselves.
- Check a person's details before you describe them as part of a community.
- Check which networks are included, so you know where the connections come from.

<Note>
**Good to know.** O works out these groups from the information available in your network. People may describe their own communities differently.
</Note>
