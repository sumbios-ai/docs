The page also shows which networks it includes and the goals you have shared with O.

## Where to find it

Select Insights in the sidebar.

<Frame caption="Insights → Overview">
  <img src="/images/insights/overview.png" alt="The Insights overview: your relationship briefing, the Network memory card, and the Who needs you now and New evidence for your topics sections" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose Ask O to ask a question about your priorities. The conversation carries on in Home.
- Use the buttons at the top of the page to open Network or Circles.
- Look through reminders, chances to reconnect, introduction requests, and news on topics you follow.
- Choose Review connected sources to go to Settings → Connections.

## How to use it

- Start by checking whose network you are looking at: your own, networks your friends share with you, or both.
- Before you follow up on an update, check where it came from and when.
- Choose an action in a section to learn more or to ask O about it.

<Note>
**Good to know.** This summary is built from the information you have brought into O. It can't show every interaction, and it doesn't guess to fill in missing emails or calendar history.
</Note>
