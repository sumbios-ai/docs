The page shows which networks are included and the goals you have shared with O.

## Where to find it

Select Insights in the sidebar.

## What you can do

- Choose Ask O to send a question about your priorities and continue on Home.
- Open Network or Circles from the buttons at the top of the page.
- Browse reminders, opportunities to reconnect, introduction requests, and updates on topics you follow.
- Use Review connected sources to open Settings → Connections.

## How to use it

- First, check whether you are looking at your own network, networks shared by friends, or both.
- Check the source and date of an update before deciding to follow up.
- Choose an action on a section to learn more or ask O about it.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** This summary uses the information you have brought into O. It cannot show every interaction, and it does not fill in missing emails or calendar history by guessing.
</Note>
