Open a notification to see the conversation, item, or page it refers to, then decide whether you want to follow up.

## Where to find it

Select Notifications in the sidebar, then choose the Notifications tab.

<Frame caption="Notifications (names blurred)">
  <img src="/images/notifications/notifications.png" alt="The Notifications tab: a friend request accepted, profile updates and sync results, each with its type and when it happened" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Open a notification to visit its related item.
- Use a notification’s menu to mark it or manage it, when those options are shown.

## How to use it

- Read the related item before responding so you know what the update is about.
- If you are expecting an update that is not listed, check the relevant page directly.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Not everything that happens in O creates a notification. This list helps you catch up on updates, but it is not a complete history of activity.
</Note>
