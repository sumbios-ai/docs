Open a notification to see the conversation, item or page it's about, then decide whether to follow up.

## Where to find it

Choose Notifications in the sidebar, then the Notifications tab.

<Frame caption="Notifications (names blurred)">
  <img src="/images/notifications/notifications.png" alt="The Notifications tab: a friend request accepted, profile updates and notices that your connections finished updating, each with its type and when it happened" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Open a notification to go to what it's about.
- Use a notification's menu to mark or manage it, when those options are shown.

## How to use it

- Before you respond, read what the notification points to, so you know what the update is about.
- If an update you expect isn't listed, go to the page it would be about and check there.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Not everything that happens in O creates a notification. This list helps you catch up on updates, but it isn't a full record of everything that has happened.
</Note>
