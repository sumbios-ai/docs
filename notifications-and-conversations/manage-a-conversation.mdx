---
title: "Manage a conversation"
description: "A conversation shows its messages and the people in it."
---

The menu you see depends on the kind of conversation.

## Where to find it

Choose Chat on the person or item the conversation is about, or open the conversation from the list, when there is one.

## What you can do

- Check who is in the conversation before you send a message.
- Write in the message box, then send when you're ready.
- Open the conversation's options to manage it or leave it, when they're shown.

## How to use it

- Keep the conversation on the topic everyone is there for.
- When you open a group conversation, check the names of the people in it.
- Check Notifications for updates, then go back to the conversation to reply.

<Note>
**Privacy.** Everyone in the conversation can see the messages. Keep personal notes in Notes, and use Home to ask O for private help.
</Note>
