See when each reminder is due and who or what it relates to, then open it to review your next step.

## Where to find it

Open Notifications → Reminders or /notifications?tab=reminders.

<Frame caption="Notifications → Reminders, before you have any">
  <img src="/images/notifications/reminders-empty.png" alt="The Reminders tab with filters All, Pending and Completed, and the empty state" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Check when a reminder is due and the member or task it is about.
- Open a reminder to read the details or mark the follow-up complete.
- Use available snooze, complete, edit, or delete actions shown on the item.

## How to use it

- Check who the reminder is about and what you planned to do before acting.
- Update a due date when your plan changes rather than leaving an inaccurate reminder.
- Use the direct reminder link to locate the specified item.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Reminder content is personal workspace data. Actions may be limited by ownership or permissions.
</Note>
