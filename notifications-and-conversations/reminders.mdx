See when each reminder is due and who or what it's about. Open one to check your next step.

## Where to find it

In Notifications, open the Reminders tab.

<Frame caption="Notifications → Reminders, before you have any">
  <img src="/images/notifications/reminders-empty.png" alt="The Reminders tab with filters All, Pending and Completed, and the empty state" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Check when a reminder is due and which person or task it's about.
- Open a reminder to read the details or mark the follow-up as complete.
- Snooze, complete, edit or delete a reminder with the options shown on it.

## How to use it

- Before you act, check who the reminder is about and what you planned to do.
- If your plan changes, update the due date so the reminder stays accurate.
- Use the link in a reminder to go straight to the item it's about.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Your reminders are personal information in your workspace. Some actions may not be open to you, depending on who owns a reminder and what you're allowed to do.
</Note>
