Some offer extra choices, such as which calendars or conversations to include.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Connections.

<Frame caption="Settings → Connections">
  <img src="/images/settings/connections.png" alt="The Integrations list: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and WhatsApp with Connect buttons, others marked Coming Soon or Upgrade Plan" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a service to connect or reconnect, then review the permission request.
- Disconnect a source using its offered action.
- Choose which calendars are included when Calendar Selection is available.
- Use a connected service’s conversation history options to choose what to bring into O.

## How to use it

- Read which information the service is asking you to share before agreeing.
- After returning to O, check that the source reports connected.
- Use the filters to include only the conversations you want O to use.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Connections may be locked by plan or administrator policy. Only connect accounts you are authorized to use; imported messages and calendars can contain sensitive information.
</Note>
