Some connections give you extra choices, such as which calendars or conversations to include.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Connections.

<Frame caption="Settings → Connections">
  <img src="/images/settings/connections.png" alt="The Integrations list: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and WhatsApp each with a Connect button, and others marked Coming Soon or Upgrade Plan" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a service to connect or reconnect, then read what it asks permission for.
- Disconnect an account using the option it shows.
- When Calendar Selection is offered, choose which calendars to include.
- For a connected service, use its conversation history options to choose what to bring into O.

## How to use it

- Before you agree, read what information the service is asking you to share.
- When you come back to O, check that the account shows as connected.
- Use the filters to include only the conversations you want O to use.

<Note>
**Good to know.** A connection may be locked by your plan or by rules your administrator has set. Only connect accounts you're allowed to use. Messages and calendars you bring in can hold sensitive information.
</Note>
