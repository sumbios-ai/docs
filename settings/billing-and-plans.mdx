Before you confirm a change, check the price and how it affects what you can use in O.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Billing &amp; Plans.

## What you can do

- See your current plan and billing details.
- Change your plan or manage how you pay, using the option shown to you.
- Check the details on the checkout confirmation before you confirm.

## How to use it

- On the confirmation screen, check the price, when the plan renews, and any change to what you can use.
- If checkout brings you back to Settings, read the message that tells you whether it went through or was cancelled.

<Note>
**Good to know.** The billing options you see depend on your account. Only enter payment details on the checkout page, never in the chat.
</Note>
