Before confirming a change, review the price and what it means for your access.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Billing &amp; Plans.

## What you can do

- Review your current plan and billing details.
- Use the offered plan-change or payment-management action.
- Review checkout confirmation details before confirming.

## How to use it

- Check price, renewal timing, and any access changes on the confirmation screen.
- If checkout returns to Settings, read the success or cancellation message.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Your account determines which billing options are available. Enter payment details only on the checkout page, never in Feedback or chat.
</Note>
