You can also review voice input, sounds, and follow-up options. Some text fields save automatically after a short pause.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Chat.

## What you can do

- Choose a tone such as Professional, Casual, Friendly, or Direct.
- Describe your role or networking goals when you want that context available to O.
- Review speech-to-text and follow-up preferences before turning them on.
- Watch for the Saving and Saved indicators after changing a text field.

## How to use it

- Keep personal notes appropriate for the assistance you request.
- Read each preference description before changing it, especially scheduled or proactive follow-ups.
- Start a new chat when you want a separate conversation; changing tone does not grant access to new data.

## Proactive follow-ups

Choose topics you want O to revisit. When O finds new information on one of them, it shares an update with you in O.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on follow-up updates">
    Open **Settings → Chat** and turn on **Follow-up updates**. Checks and updates stay paused until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a topic">
    Under **Topics**, choose **Add topic** and describe what you want to follow, for example a company's product launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start and stop following a topic from the chat. See [Ask O to keep an eye on something](/getting-started/home#ask-o-to-keep-an-eye-on-something).

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Preferences affect chat behavior but do not expand data permissions. Exact options can vary by account.
</Note>
