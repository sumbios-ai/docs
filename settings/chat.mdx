Here you can also look at the settings for voice input, sounds and follow-ups. Some text boxes save on their own a moment after you stop typing.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Chat.

<Frame caption="Settings → Chat">
  <img src="/images/settings/chat.png" alt="The Chat section in Settings, with Tone of Voice, Your Role and What you want help with" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Pick a tone, such as Professional, Casual, Friendly, or Direct.
- Describe your role or your networking goals if you want O to know them.
- Read the voice typing and follow-up settings before you turn them on.
- After you change a text box, look for the Saving and Saved messages.

## How to use it

- Only add personal details that fit the help you are asking for.
- Read the description of each setting before you change it, especially for scheduled or proactive follow-ups.
- Start a new chat when you want a separate conversation. Changing the tone doesn't give O access to any new information.

## Proactive follow-ups

Choose topics you want O to keep checking. When O finds something new on one of them, it shares an update with you in O.

<Frame caption="Settings → Chat → Proactive follow-ups">
  <img src="/images/settings/chat-proactive-follow-ups.png" alt="The Proactive follow-ups section: the Follow-up updates switch, a box for a topic, and Add topic" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on follow-up updates">
    Open **Settings → Chat** and turn on **Follow-up updates**. Checks and updates stay paused until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a topic">
    Under **Topics**, choose **Add topic** and describe what you want to follow, for example a company's product launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start and stop following a topic from the chat. See [Ask O to keep an eye on something](/getting-started/home#ask-o-to-keep-an-eye-on-something).

<Note>
**Good to know.** These settings change how O chats with you. They don't give O access to more of your information. The exact options can differ from one account to another.
</Note>
