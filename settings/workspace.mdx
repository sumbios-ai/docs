Start Page lets you choose which page opens first. Visible Areas lets you pick which sections appear in the side menu, out of the ones you can use.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Workspace.

<Frame caption="Settings → Workspace">
  <img src="/images/settings/workspace.png" alt="The Start Page and Visible Areas settings" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose the page you want to open first.
- In the side menu, show the sections you use and hide the ones you don't need.
- Save or confirm your choice when asked.

## How to use it

- Keep Home visible, and pick a start page you have access to.
- If a section is missing, check whether you hid it here or whether your plan doesn't include it.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Hiding a section only takes it out of the side menu. Your work in it isn't deleted. Some sections may not be part of your plan.
</Note>
