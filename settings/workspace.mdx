Start Page lets you choose where to begin, and Visible Areas lets you decide which available sections appear in the side menu.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Workspace.

<Frame caption="Settings → Workspace">
  <img src="/images/settings/workspace.png" alt="Start page and visible areas" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a page you would like to open first.
- Show the sections you use and hide the ones you do not need in the side menu.
- Save or confirm the preference when prompted.

## How to use it

- Keep Home visible; choose a landing page you can access.
- If a section is missing, check whether it is hidden here or unavailable on your plan.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Hiding a section only removes it from the side menu; it does not delete your work. Some sections may not be available on your plan.
</Note>
