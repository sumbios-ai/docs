Read the current program details to understand who can use your referral and when any credits apply.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Referrals &amp; Credits or use the Referrals &amp; Credits sidebar shortcut.

## What you can do

- Review the displayed referral link or code and program terms.
- Use the copy/share action if provided.
- Check the displayed credit balance or status.

## How to use it

- Read eligibility and expiry conditions before sharing.
- Confirm a credit is applied in the account before relying on it.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Referral eligibility, credit value, and terms can change. The displayed program terms are authoritative.
</Note>
