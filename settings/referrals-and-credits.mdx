Read the current program details to see who can use your referral and when credits apply.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Referrals &amp; Credits, or use the Referrals &amp; Credits shortcut in the side menu.

## What you can do

- See your referral link or code, and the program terms.
- Use the copy or share button, if there is one.
- Check your credit balance or status.

## How to use it

- Before you share, read the rules on who qualifies and when things expire.
- Before you count on a credit, check that it shows on your account.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Who qualifies, what a credit is worth and the terms can all change. The program terms shown in O are the ones that count.
</Note>
