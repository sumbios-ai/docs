Choose a light or dark look, try a color theme and change the text size. You can also decide how the windows at the side of the screen behave, and whether a daily quote appears.

## Where to find it

Open Settings from your user menu or the app menu, then choose Appearance.

<Frame caption="Settings → Appearance">
  <img src="/images/settings/appearance.png" alt="The Appearance settings: light or dark mode and visual style" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose Light, Dark, or System.
- Pick a visual style, and narrow down the color themes by type.
- Use the slider to make the text bigger or smaller.
- Set your Dock and Daily Quote choices. Use Reset to go back to the original settings.

## How to use it

- Try one change at a time and check how it looks in the preview or on the page.
- To find a color theme, use the type buttons: All, Minimal, Colorful, Branded, Creative and Dark.
- Unpin on Close is still being tested. When it's on, closing a pinned tab at the side of the screen moves that tab into a floating window. It doesn't apply to the Assistant.

<Note>
**Good to know.** The themes you can pick depend on what the app currently offers. Changing how O looks doesn't change any of your information.
</Note>
