Review the relationship goals you chose when getting started, add a current focus, or set an optional Reach target for Network Health.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Goals.

<Frame caption="Settings → Goals">
  <img src="/images/settings/goals.png" alt="The Relationship Goals pane, with six goal cards and four selected" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Toggle the goal cards that describe what you want O to help with.
- Enter a current focus in the text area when useful.
- Set a positive whole-number Reach target within the displayed limit, or leave it empty.
- Select Save to update goals and preferences.

## How to use it

- Review the saved state after the update confirmation.
- Change goals as your priorities change; the optional target is not a guaranteed outcome.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Goal settings inform context but do not guarantee specific recommendations or outcomes.
</Note>
