Look back at the relationship goals you picked when you started, add what you're focused on now, or set a Reach target for Network Health if you want one.

## Where to find it

Open Settings → Goals.

<Frame caption="Settings → Goals">
  <img src="/images/settings/goals.png" alt="The Relationship Goals section, with six goal cards and four selected" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Select or clear the goal cards that describe what you want O to help with.
- Type what you're focused on right now in the text box, if it helps.
- Set a Reach target as a whole number above zero, up to the limit shown, or leave it empty.
- Select Save to update your goals and preferences.

## How to use it

- When you see the confirmation, check that your changes were saved.
- Change your goals when your priorities change. The Reach target is optional, and setting one doesn't guarantee you'll reach it.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Your goals give O background on what matters to you. They don't guarantee particular suggestions or results.
</Note>
