Where you see a question box, describe what you're looking for in your own words. O can turn that into a search and show you the results.

## Where to find it

In Discovery, open Search history. Or, when it's shown, type your request in the plain-language search box.

## What you can do

- Choose a past search to bring back what you searched for and its results.
- Remove a single past search with its remove option.
- In the plain-language search box, ask clearly for the people or companies you want.

## How to use it

- Before you run the search or use its results, check how O understood your question.
- Be specific about names, locations and companies.
- Close the search history to make more room for the results.

<Note>
**Privacy.** Your search history may include the names and other details you searched with. Don't put sensitive personal details in a search unless you need them.
</Note>
