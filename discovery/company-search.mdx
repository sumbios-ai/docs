---
title: "Search for companies"
description: "Look for a company by name, industry, website address or location."
---

Results can show the company's website, size, headquarters, and whether it's already in your network.

## Where to find it

Choose Discovery, then the Companies tab.

<Frame caption="Discovery → Companies">
  <img src="/images/discovery/company-search.png" alt="Company search: company name, industry and size, then Review search credits" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Type a company name, or use the filters to describe the kind of company you want.
- When there's a link, open the company's website or profile for a closer look.
- Choose Add to bring a company into your network, then check the confirmation.
- Use Search history to reopen or remove an earlier company search.

## How to use it

- If several companies have similar names, add a website address or location.
- Check the company's website and profile before you add it.
- Before you confirm, check which company you're adding and what adding it will do.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Company searches use credits. Different search services find different companies and details. For up-to-date information, check the company's own website or profile.
</Note>
