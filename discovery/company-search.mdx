---
title: "Search for companies"
description: "Look for a company by name, industry, website address, or location."
---

Results can include its website, size, headquarters, and whether it already appears in your network.

## Where to find it

Select Discovery, then choose Companies search mode/tab.

## What you can do

- Enter a company name or use the filters to describe the kind of company you are looking for.
- Open the company’s website or profile to take a closer look, when a link is available.
- Choose Add to bring a company into your network, then review the confirmation.
- Use Search history to reopen or remove a previous company search.

## How to use it

- Add a website address or location if several companies have similar names.
- Check the company website and profile before adding it.
- Before confirming, check which company you are adding and what the action will do.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Company searches use credits. Search services differ in the companies and details they can find. Check the original website or profile for current information.
</Note>
