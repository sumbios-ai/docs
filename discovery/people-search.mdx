---
title: "Search for people"
description: "Look for people beyond the information already in your network."
---

In the Person tab, search by name, role, company, or location. Each result shows the details available and whether the person is already in your network.

## Where to find it

Select Discovery, then choose the People search mode/tab.

## What you can do

- Enter what you know about the person you are looking for, then search.
- Read the results and open a person’s original profile when a link is available.
- Choose Add if you want to bring a result into your network, then review the confirmation.
- Open Search history to restore a prior people search or remove an entry.

## How to use it

- Start with a name and add company or location to distinguish similar people.
- Check that you have found the right person before adding them.
- In the confirmation window, check which profile you are adding and where it will go.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Discovery searches use credits. Before you start a search, O shows how many profiles match and how many credits the search will use. The searches available depend on your plan and connected search services. A result may be incomplete or out of date, so check the original profile.
</Note>
