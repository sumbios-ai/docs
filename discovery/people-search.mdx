In the Person tab, search by name, role, company or location. Each result shows whatever details are available, and whether the person is already in your network.

## Where to find it

Choose Discovery, then the People tab.

<Frame caption="Discovery → People">
  <img src="/images/discovery/people-search.png" alt="People search: describe who you want to find, or fill in name, job title and company, then Check matches" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Type what you know about the person you're looking for, then search.
- Read the results. When there's a link, open the person's original profile.
- To bring someone into your network, choose Add, then check the confirmation.
- Open Search history to bring back an earlier people search or remove one.

## How to use it

- Start with a name, then add a company or location to tell similar people apart.
- Make sure you've found the right person before you add them.
- In the confirmation window, check which profile you're adding and where it will go.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Discovery searches use credits. Before you start a search, O shows how many profiles match and how many credits the search will use. Which searches you can run depends on your plan and on which search services are connected. A result may be incomplete or out of date, so check the person's original profile.
</Note>
