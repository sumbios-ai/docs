O works with what you choose to bring in. Each source is connected by you, through the provider's own sign-in, and can be disconnected at any time from **Settings → Connections**.

| Source | What O can use | How you connect it |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Your connections, and the profiles you open | The [O browser extension](/getting-started/sign-in-and-get-started) |
| Google Workspace | Gmail, Calendar, and Contacts | Settings → Connections |
| Microsoft 365 | Outlook, Calendar, and Contacts | Settings → Connections |
| WhatsApp | Your WhatsApp messages | Settings → Connections |

More integrations, including CRM, HR and document tools, are available on some plans. Settings → Connections shows which ones your plan includes.

## Before you connect

- Read what the provider asks you to share before you agree.
- After you return to O, check that the source shows as connected.
- Where a source offers it, choose which calendars or conversations O may use.

See [Connections](/settings/connections) and [Communication Memory](/insights/communication-memory) for the details.
