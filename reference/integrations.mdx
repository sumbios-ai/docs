O works with what you choose to bring in. You connect each account yourself, through that service's own sign-in, and you can disconnect it at any time from **Settings → Connections**.

| Account | What O can use | How you connect it |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Your connections, and the profiles you open | The [O browser extension](/getting-started/sign-in-and-get-started), an add-on for your web browser |
| Google Workspace | Gmail, Calendar, and Contacts | Settings → Connections |
| Microsoft 365 | Outlook, Calendar, and Contacts | Settings → Connections |
| WhatsApp | Your WhatsApp messages | Settings → Connections |

Some plans include more connections, such as customer relationship (CRM) tools, HR tools and document tools. Settings → Connections shows which ones your plan includes.

## Before you connect

- Before you agree, read what the service asks you to share.
- When you come back to O, check that the account shows as connected.
- If the account gives you the choice, pick which calendars or conversations O may use.

For more detail, see [Connections](/settings/connections) and [Communication Memory](/insights/communication-memory).
