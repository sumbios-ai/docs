title: "What you can ask O"
description: "Everything you can ask O to do, with an example you can copy for each."
---

You can ask O in your own words, or start with a command from the `/` menu. Each section below lists what O can do, an example to copy, and the command if there is one. Swap the bracketed parts for your own, and type `@` to pick the exact person, company or Circle.

For tips on phrasing, see [How to ask O](/getting-started/how-to-ask).

## People and companies

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Find people in your network by role, company, skill, location, country, school, industry, language or certification | *Find product leaders in Amsterdam who used to work at @[company].* | `/people` |
| Find people by seniority, by whether they make decisions or manage a team, or by whether they're open to work | *Which senior decision-makers do I know in fintech?* | `/people` |
| Find people by when you connected or how many connections they have | *Who did I connect with this year?* | `/people` |
| Look up one person | *Tell me about @[person]: what they do now and what we have in common.* | `/person` |
| Find companies by name or industry, by where they're based, and sort them by size, growth, hiring or how many people you know there | *Which fintech companies based in the Nordics do I know the most people at?* | `/company` |
| Get a briefing on one company | *Give me a briefing on @[company].* | `/company-info` |
| See a company's leaders, current team and former team | *Who is on the leadership team at @[company], and who has left in the last year?* | |
| See confirmed changes of role or company among your contacts | *Who in my network has changed role or company this month?* | |

## Your relationships

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Find the best person to introduce you to someone | *What is the best person to introduce me to @[person]?* | `/intro` |
| Find people in your network who should meet each other | *Which people in my network should meet each other? Look for skills that fit together.* | `/matches` |
| Draft an introduction between two people | *Draft an introduction between @[person] and @[person] about [purpose].* | `/intro-draft` |
| Find people to get back in touch with | *Who in my network should I reconnect with?* | |
| See your history with someone: emails, meetings, messages, notes and reminders | *Show me my history with @[person].* | |
| See how much O can tell about your relationship with someone | *How well do I know @[person], based on what you can see?* | |
| Find people who may know each other without you knowing | *Who in my network might know each other?* | |

How well you know someone is O's reading of the evidence it has: shared employers, schools, skills, mutual connections, messages, emails, meetings, notes and how recent they are. It doesn't measure trust, or whether someone would be willing to help.

## Your messages, calendar, notes and documents

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Search your email, calendar events and LinkedIn messages, by person, company or date | *What did @[person] and I talk about in September?* | `/content` |
| See what's in your calendar, with whom | *What meetings do I have next week, and with whom?* | |
| Search your notes about a person, company or topic | *What have I written about @[company]?* | `/note` |
| Add a note to a person or company | *Add a note to @[person]: met at the summit, interested in a pilot.* | |
| Search the documents you've added | *What does my document on pricing say about discounts?* | `/memory` |
| Ask questions about a spreadsheet you've added: counts, totals, averages, filters and groups | *In my spreadsheet of deals, how many are over 100,000, by country?* | `/memory` |
| See which accounts you've connected | *Which accounts have I connected?* | `/list-integrations` |

When O finds the right email or message, ask it to read the full text if you need the details.

## Your network as a whole

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Compare two or more people | *Compare @[person] and @[person]: skills, experience and education.* | `/compare` |
| See your network in groups, for example by industry, company, location, skills, school or seniority | *Group my network by industry.* | `/cluster` |
| See how your network has grown | *How has my network grown each quarter?* | `/growth` |
| See where people at a company came from, or where they went | *Where do people who leave @[company] go next?* | |
| See what has changed in your network | *What has changed in my network in the last two weeks?* | |
| Make a chart | *Make a pie chart of my network by industry.* | `/chart` |
| Draw a diagram, such as a timeline or an introduction path | *Draw the path from me to @[person].* | |
| Turn an answer into a PDF report | *Turn this answer into a PDF report.* | `/export` |
| Find skills your network is missing | *Which of these skills is my network missing: data, security, design?* | `/skill-gap` |
| Find companies in your network that could suit someone | *Which companies in my network would suit @[person]?* | |
| Check a list against your network | *Attach a list of companies, then ask: Who do I know at each of these?* | |

## Circles

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Add people to a Circle | *Add these people to my Circle @[circle].* | `/add-to-circle` |
| Create a Circle, see who's in one, or remove someone | *Create a Circle called [name] with the people above.* | |

O shows you any change to a Circle first, and makes it only when you confirm.

## Research and news

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Do a quick search of the web | *Search the web for @[company]'s latest funding round.* | `/websearch` |
| Research a person or company in depth | *Research @[company]: funding, leadership and recent news.* | `/research` |
| Get the news about a company or topic | *What's in the news about @[company] this week?* | `/news` |

O only searches the web when you ask it to.

## Drafts, sending and reminders

| What you can do | Ask like this | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Draft a LinkedIn message or an email to someone | *Draft a short LinkedIn message to @[person] asking for 20 minutes.* | `/outreach` |
| Send an email from your Google or Outlook account | *Email @[person] to confirm Thursday at 10.* | |
| Send a WhatsApp message to someone you already have a WhatsApp chat with | *Send @[person] a WhatsApp: running 10 minutes late.* | |
| Book a meeting in your calendar | *Book 30 minutes with @[person] next Tuesday at 10.* | |
| Set a reminder, once or on repeat | *Remind me to follow up with @[person] every Monday.* | `/remind` |
| Keep an eye on a topic | *Keep an eye on @[company]'s launch.* | |
| Tailor your résumé to a job description | *Tailor my résumé to this job description.* | `/resume` |

<Note>
**Nothing goes out without you.** Before O sends an email or a WhatsApp message, or books a meeting, it shows you exactly what will go out and waits for you to confirm. Booking a meeting doesn't check whether people are free. Keeping an eye on a topic needs **Follow-up updates** turned on; see [Proactive follow-ups](/settings/chat#proactive-follow-ups).
</Note>

## Good to know

- O works with what you've connected and added. If an account isn't connected, or a profile doesn't list something, O can't see it.
- A missing detail is unknown, not false. Profiles and company details can be out of date.
- One answer can only list so many people. If you need more, split the question.
