Search for a topic, open a result, and check the original information when you want to learn more.

## Where to find it

Open Notes → Wiki when the Wiki tab is enabled.

<Frame caption="Notes → Wiki">
  <img src="/images/notes-and-knowledge/wiki.png" alt="The Wiki overview, with Current Coverage and Opportunity" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Browse the pages or search for a topic you are interested in.
- Open a result to read the information behind it.
- Use the actions offered on a page when you want to work with its content.

## How to use it

- Search with a distinctive topic or term.
- Check the underlying source before reusing a statement.
- Return to Notes for personal note editing.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** You can only browse material available to your account that has been added to O’s knowledge collection. Some documents may be out of date or read-only, so check before using or changing them.
</Note>
