Search for a topic and open a result. To learn more, check the original information it comes from.

## Where to find it

In Notes, open the Wiki tab if you see it.

<Frame caption="Notes → Wiki">
  <img src="/images/notes-and-knowledge/wiki.png" alt="The Wiki overview, with Current Coverage and Opportunity" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Browse the pages or search for a topic that interests you.
- Open a result to read the information behind it.
- Use the options on a page to work with what's on it.

## How to use it

- Search for a specific topic or word.
- Before you reuse something you read here, check where it came from.
- To edit your own notes, go back to Notes.

<Note>
**Good to know.** You can only browse material that your account can see and that has been added to O's collection of knowledge. Some documents may be out of date, or you may not be able to change them, so check before you use or edit them.
</Note>
