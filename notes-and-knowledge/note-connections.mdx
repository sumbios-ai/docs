Follow a line to see what is related, then open a note to read the details.

## Where to find it

In Notes, open the Note Connections tab.

<Frame caption="Notes → Note Connections">
  <img src="/images/notes-and-knowledge/note-connections.png" alt="Note Connections with its legend: note, person, company, similar content, same entity, linked to" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a note or item on the map to see its details.
- Move around and zoom in to look at a group of related notes.
- Use the filters or search, when shown, to focus on one note or one link.

## How to use it

- Start with a note you know, then follow the lines from it to other items.
- Open the note itself to check why two items are linked.
- Go back to Notes when you want to edit a note or its properties.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Some lines come from information saved in O. Others are suggestions O has found. A line on the map doesn't prove that something is true or that two people know each other.
</Note>
