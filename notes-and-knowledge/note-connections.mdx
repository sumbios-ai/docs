Follow a connection to explore related information, then open a note to understand the details.

## Where to find it

Open Notes → Note Connections.

<Frame caption="Notes → Note Connections">
  <img src="/images/notes-and-knowledge/note-connections.png" alt="Note Connections with its legend: note, person, company, similar content, same entity, linked to" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose a note or item on the map to see its details.
- Move around and zoom in to explore a group of related notes.
- Use available filters or search to focus on a note or relationship.

## How to use it

- Start with a note you recognize and follow the lines connecting it to other items.
- Open the underlying note to verify why two items are linked.
- Return to Notes when you want to edit a note or its properties.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Some connections come from saved information; others are suggestions O has found. A line on the map does not prove that a statement is true or that two people know each other.
</Note>
