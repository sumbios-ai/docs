Library holds your notes and documents. In Shared with you, you'll find what others have shared. Note Connections shows how your notes link to people and other items.

## Where to find it

Choose Notes in the sidebar. Its tabs are Library, Shared with you, Note Connections and Wiki.

<Frame caption="Notes → Library">
  <img src="/images/notes-and-knowledge/notes.png" alt="The Notes library with search, filters and New note" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Create a note with Add or New Note.
- Search your notes, narrow the list with filters, or pick the view you like.
- Open Shared with you to see notes others have shared and what you can do with each one.
- Open Note Connections to see how your notes link to each other and to related items.

## How to use it

- Use the filters to find what you need. Clear a filter when you want to see more notes.
- Choose a note to read or edit it, or open its properties to see details such as labels.
- The note menu lets you rename, export or download, refresh or delete a note, when those options are shown.
- Before you edit a shared note, check that you are allowed to.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Only some accounts have Wiki. The person who shares a note decides whether you can edit it. Before you add private information to a note, check who can see it.
</Note>
