Library holds your notes and documents, Shared with you shows what others have shared, and Note Connections helps you explore how your notes relate to people and other items.

## Where to find it

Select Notes in the sidebar. The page tabs are Library, Shared with you, Note Connections, and Wiki.

## What you can do

- Create a note with Add/New Note.
- Search your notes, narrow the list with filters, or choose a view that suits you.
- Open Shared with you to review shared notes and their permission.
- Open Note Connections to explore links between notes and related items.

## How to use it

- Use the filters to find what you need. Remove a selected filter when you want to see more notes.
- Choose a note to read or edit it, or open its properties to review details such as labels.
- Use the note menu for available rename, export/download, refresh, or delete actions.
- Check permissions before editing a shared note.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Wiki is available on some accounts. The person sharing a note decides whether you can edit it. Check who can see a note before adding private information.
</Note>
