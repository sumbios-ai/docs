Copy any prompt below and paste it into Ask O. Replace the words in square brackets with your own. Type `@` before the name of a person or a company so O finds them in your network.

## Due diligence on a partner, with an honest view from people you know

**For:** Corporate development, Investor. **When:** A partnership is serious enough that you want to check it independently, before you sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA).

```text wrap
I'm evaluating a potential partnership with @[company]. Run due diligence: who runs it, who backs it, who their key partners and clients are, and any red flags. Then tell me who in my network is connected to any of those companies and who can give me a warm, honest read.
```

## Company diligence before investing

**For:** Investor. **When:** An investment is on the table and you need the full picture, and to know what is missing from it.

```text wrap
I'm evaluating an investment in @[company]. Run due diligence: who runs it, who backs it, who sits on the board, key customers and partners, and any governance red flags. Then tell me who in my network is connected to any of those people or companies and who can give me a candid, off-the-record read.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Lists what it could not confirm as things to check (for example, no public list of board members, or funding rounds reported differently in different places), and ranks who in your network can fill each gap. **Saves you:** 2 to 3 h.

## A reference on the leadership before we invest

**For:** Investor. **When:** You're keeping the founder or CEO in their job and want an honest opinion of them.

```text wrap
We're about to back @[company] and keep @[person] in place. Map their career and every company behind it, then tell me who in my network overlapped with them and would give me an honest read on how they actually operate.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Maps their career, then finds the people who worked with them at each step and says what each one is best placed to tell you. **Saves you:** 1 to 2 h.

## The honest reference before I hire

**For:** HR. **When:** You're about to send a job offer.

```text wrap
I'm about to make an offer to @[person] for [role]. Map their career and the companies behind it, then tell me who in my network overlapped with them and who would give me a candid, off-the-record read.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Off-the-record references from your own network, found in minutes instead of over a week of asking around. **Saves you:** 1 to 2 h.

## Map the sister companies

**For:** Corporate development, Investor. **When:** The company you're looking at is part of a complicated group of companies with shared owners.

```text wrap
Do a deep research behind @[company]: find all of their partner and sibling companies, the shared board members, and see who in my network works in any of them.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Finds the board members shared across the group, then the people you know inside it. **Saves you:** 2 to 3 h.

## Who is this person, really

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You've been sent a name and nothing else.

```text wrap
Who is @[person]? Career, companies, boards, what they have said publicly this year, and every connection between us. Flag anything I should know before I reply.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Their full profile and what you have in common, with anything awkward shown first. **Saves you:** 30 min.

## What is a fund actually looking for

**For:** Investor, Founder. **When:** Before you pitch to them or invest alongside them.

```text wrap
What has @[fund] invested in over the last two years, at what stage and cheque size, and what does the pattern say they are looking for now? Who in my network has taken money from them?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Looks at the pattern in the deals they actually made, not at what their website says. **Saves you:** 1 to 2 h.
