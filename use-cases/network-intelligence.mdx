Copy any prompt below and paste it into Ask O. Replace the words in square brackets with your own. Type `@` before the name of a person or a company so O finds them in your network.

## Review your week

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Friday. You want to know what happened, who you met, and what carries over to next week.

```text wrap
It's the end of the week. Do a weekly review of my conversations, messages, email and calendar, and who I connected with, so I get a 360 view of new connections and what to keep top of mind for next week.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Reads all the places you talk to people and writes the review you would not have written yourself. **Saves you:** 30 to 60 min.

## My network is going stale

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Once a month, before losing touch costs you.

```text wrap
Show me the ten people in my network I should reconnect with this month, with a specific, non-generic reason for each based on what has changed on their side.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Each reason comes from what changed for them (a new role, money raised, a company sold), not from how long it has been since you spoke. **Saves you:** 1 h.

## Work out the network of this company

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You want to understand an organisation through the people who joined it and left it.

```text wrap
What is the inferred network of @[company]? Show me the alumni movement: where people came from, where they went, and which of them I can reach.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Shows the organisation as people coming and going over time, not as a chart of who reports to whom. **Saves you:** 2 h.

## Summarise my network

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You want a quick overview for yourself or a partner.

```text wrap
Create a summary of my network: the most influential people, where it is strong and where it is thin, and what I should do about it.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Sets out the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats in your relationships (a SWOT), with the names behind each finding. **Saves you:** 2 h.

## Who did I connect with, and what does it say

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Every couple of weeks.

```text wrap
Who did I connect with in the past two weeks? Give me a table with their industries and companies, and tell me what pattern you see.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** A table, plus what it tells you as a whole. **Saves you:** 20 min.

## Who should I introduce to each other

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You want to be useful to the people you know.

```text wrap
Inside my @[circle], who are the five pairs of people I should introduce to each other, where their backgrounds fill gaps in each other's work?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Finds pairs whose backgrounds fill each other's gaps, with the reason to make each introduction. **Saves you:** 1 h.

## Who did I meet at that event

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Months later, there's a name you can't place.

```text wrap
Who did I meet at [event] on [dates]? What did we talk about, and did anything come of it?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Puts your calendar, messages and the dates you connected into one answer. **Saves you:** 30 min.

## Prepare my week

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Monday morning.

```text wrap
Help me prepare for the week ahead. Go through my emails, calendar and messages: what is coming, who do I need to get back to, and where should I spend my attention?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** A plan built from your own email, calendar and messages, not a to-do list you typed. **Saves you:** 45 min.

## Where my network is thin

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You're planning which relationships to work on over the next three months.

```text wrap
Given what I'm building at [company], where is my network strong and where is it thin? Who are the ten people I should establish a relationship with, and who can get me to each?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Shows where your network falls short of your goal, and how to reach the people who would fill the gap. **Saves you:** 2 h.

## Set a reminder on a relationship

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You promised to follow up.

```text wrap
Remind me [tomorrow at 16:00] to reach out to @[person] about [topic].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** The reminder is attached to the relationship, so you get the background with it when it comes up. **Saves you:** a forgotten promise.
