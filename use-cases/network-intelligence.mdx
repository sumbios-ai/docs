Each prompt is ready to paste into Ask O. Swap the bracketed parts for your own, and mention people and companies with `@` so O finds them in your network.

## Review your week

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Friday. What happened, who did you meet, what carries over.

```text wrap
It's the end of the week. Do a weekly review of my conversations, messages, email and calendar, and who I connected with, so I get a 360 view of new connections and what to keep top of mind for next week.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Reads across every channel and writes the review you would not have written. **Time saved:** 30 to 60 min.

## My network is going stale

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Once a month, before it costs you.

```text wrap
Show me the ten people in my network I should reconnect with this month, with a specific, non-generic reason for each based on what has changed on their side.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** The reason is drawn from their side (role change, raise, exit), not from how long it has been. **Time saved:** 1 h.

## What is the inferred network of this company

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Understanding an organisation through the people who moved through it.

```text wrap
What is the inferred network of @[company]? Show me the alumni movement: where people came from, where they went, and which of them I can reach.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Draws the organisation as a flow of people over time, not an org chart. **Time saved:** 2 h.

## Summarise my network

**For:** Everyone. **When:** A snapshot for yourself or a partner.

```text wrap
Create a summary of my network: the most influential people, where it is strong and where it is thin, and what I should do about it.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** A SWOT of your own relationships, with the names behind each finding. **Time saved:** 2 h.

## Who did I connect with, and what does it say

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Every couple of weeks.

```text wrap
Who did I connect with in the past two weeks? Give me a table with their industries and companies, and tell me what pattern you see.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** A table plus the reading across it. **Time saved:** 20 min.

## Who should I introduce to each other

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Being useful to your own network.

```text wrap
Inside my @[circle], who are the five pairs of people I should introduce to each other, where their backgrounds fill gaps in each other's work?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Finds complementary pairs, with the reason to make each introduction. **Time saved:** 1 h.

## Who did I meet at that event

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Months later, a name you cannot place.

```text wrap
Who did I meet at [event] on [dates]? What did we talk about, and did anything come of it?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Calendar, messages and connection dates, stitched into one answer. **Time saved:** 30 min.

## Prepare my week

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Monday morning.

```text wrap
Help me prepare for the week ahead. Go through my emails, calendar and messages: what is coming, who do I need to get back to, and where should I spend my attention?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** A plan built from your own channels, not a to-do list you typed. **Time saved:** 45 min.

## Where my network is thin

**For:** Everyone. **When:** Planning where to spend the next quarter of relationships.

```text wrap
Given what I'm building at [company], where is my network strong and where is it thin? Who are the ten people I should establish a relationship with, and who can get me to each?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Gap analysis against your goal, with the paths to close it. **Time saved:** 2 h.

## Set a reminder on a relationship

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You promised to follow up.

```text wrap
Remind me [tomorrow at 16:00] to reach out to @[person] about [topic].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** The reminder lives with the relationship, so the context comes back with it. **Time saved:** a forgotten promise.
