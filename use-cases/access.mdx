title: "Access"
description: "Find someone you know who can introduce you to a person, a firm or a fund, and learn which people not to ask."
---

Copy any prompt below and paste it into Ask O. Replace the words in square brackets with your own. Type `@` before the name of a person or a company so O finds them in your network.

## Who can actually introduce me to this investor

**For:** Founder. **When:** You're raising money and need people who can introduce you, not a list of funds.

```text wrap
I'm raising [round] for [thesis]. Find the funds that have backed comparable companies in the last 24 months, then rank who in my network can make a genuine warm introduction to each, and who I should not use.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Ranks each way in by how strong the relationships are, and tells you which contacts are too out of touch, or too far from the decision, to use. **Saves you:** 2 to 4 h.

## Find your way into a fund

**For:** Founder, Investor. **When:** You want to reach a specific fund through people, not an email out of the blue.

```text wrap
Which companies has @[fund] invested in over the past three years? Do a deep research and find people in my network who have been part of those companies.
```

## Who can reach the partners at this firm

**For:** Founder, Investor, Corporate development. **When:** You know the firm. You need the right person there, and a way to reach them.

```text wrap
Who can reach the partners at @[firm]? First from shared work and investment history alone, then through my own network. Rank the paths and tell me which not to use.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Looks at public information first (people linked through past jobs, shared employers), then your own network, and gives you a list of paths not to use. **Saves you:** 1 to 2 h.

## Reach co-investors and fund investors (LPs)

**For:** Investor. **When:** You're building a group of investors for a deal (a syndicate), or a relationship with a firm to invest alongside.

```text wrap
Who has co-invested alongside @[firm] in the last 36 months, and which of those funds is now raising or deploying in [sector]? Rank who in my network can make a genuine warm introduction to each, and who we should not use.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Finds the funds, checks what they are doing now, then ranks your ways in. It warns you when a match only has a similar name and is not a real match. **Saves you:** 2 to 4 h.

## Who can introduce me to this person

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You have a name and no way to reach them.

```text wrap
Who can introduce me to @[person]? Rank the paths by how well I know the connector and how well they know them, and tell me which one to use first.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Weighs how well you know each connector and how well they know the person, rather than giving a list of people you both know. **Saves you:** 30 min.

## The best introducer, with /intro

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You know who you want to meet and want someone who can introduce you, in one command.

```text wrap
/intro What is the best person to introduce me to @[person]?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** The /intro command tells O to look for someone who can introduce you, so you get the one person to ask, not a list of people you both know. **Saves you:** 30 min.

## Who in my network can vouch for me at this company

**For:** Founder. **When:** You are pitching a company and want a reference they trust.

```text wrap
I'm pitching @[company]. Who in my network has worked there, sold to them, or sits on their board, and who among them would vouch for me?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Finds the people they already trust who also know you. **Saves you:** 1 h.

## Find a board member

**For:** Founder, Investor. **When:** You have a board seat to fill and know the kind of person you want.

```text wrap
Who in my network could sit on the board of [company], given we need [experience]? Rank by fit, independence and how well I know them.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Searches your own network for people who fit the board role, and checks how independent each one is. **Saves you:** 2 h.
