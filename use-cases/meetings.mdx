Copy any prompt below and paste it into Ask O. Replace the words in square brackets with your own. Type `@` before the name of a person or a company so O finds them in your network.

## Prep me for tomorrow's room

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You have a meeting on the calendar and thirty minutes to get ready.

```text wrap
I'm meeting [names] from [company] tomorrow about [topic]. Tell me who they are, what has moved at their company recently, and every real connection between us.
```

## Prepare for a conference

**For:** Founder, Investor. **When:** You have an event tomorrow with hundreds of people and no plan.

```text wrap
Tomorrow is [event]. Find the partners, speakers and companies taking part, cross them with my network, and help me prepare. My goal is to meet [investors and founders].
```

## Prep me for a partner meeting

**For:** Investor, Corporate development. **When:** You're meeting another firm about investing together (co-investment), selling a company (exit), or buying one to add to a company you already own (add-on).

```text wrap
I'm meeting [names] from [firm] tomorrow about [co-investment / exit / add-on]. Tell me who they are, what has moved in their portfolio in the last quarter, which of their deals touched ours, and every real connection between us.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Puts the recent changes in their portfolio next to yours and finds the overlaps a search would never turn up. **Saves you:** 1 to 2 h.

## What did we talk about last time

**For:** Everyone. **When:** There's a name on your calendar you only half remember.

```text wrap
@[person] is on my calendar. What did we last talk about, what did I promise, and what has changed for them since?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Reads your own emails, messages and notes to find your history, then checks what has changed for them. **Saves you:** 20 min.

## Prep for a customer call

**For:** Founder, Sales. **When:** A call with a possible customer or a design partner (an early customer who helps shape the product), and you want to know the people, not just the company.

```text wrap
I have a call with @[company] about [what]. Who is on their side, who decides, who in my network knows them, and what has the company said publicly about [topic] this year?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Maps the people involved in the buying decision and your ways to reach them, in one go. **Saves you:** 45 min.

## Draft the follow-up

**For:** Everyone. **When:** The meeting just ended.

```text wrap
I just met @[person]. Based on my notes and our history, draft a short follow-up in my tone that confirms [what we agreed] and proposes [next step].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Drafts it the way you write, using your own note on your tone, with the details of this relationship. **Saves you:** 15 min.
