Each prompt is ready to paste into Ask O. Swap the bracketed parts for your own, and mention people and companies with `@` so O finds them in your network.

## Prep me for tomorrow's room

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You have a meeting on the calendar and thirty minutes to get ready.

```text wrap
I'm meeting [names] from [company] tomorrow about [topic]. Tell me who they are, what has moved at their company recently, and every real connection between us.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Intersects their public profile and company news with your own history: shared employers, mutual contacts, the last thing you talked about. **Time saved:** 45 to 90 min.

## Prepare for a conference

**For:** Founder, Investor. **When:** An event tomorrow with hundreds of people and no plan.

```text wrap
Tomorrow is [event]. Find the partners, speakers and companies taking part, cross them with my network, and help me prepare. My goal is to meet [investors and founders].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Turns a speaker list into a ranked list of who to find, who can introduce you, and what to say. **Time saved:** 1.5 to 2 h.

## Prep me for a partner meeting

**For:** Investor, Corp dev. **When:** A co-investment, exit or add-on conversation with another firm.

```text wrap
I'm meeting [names] from [firm] tomorrow about [co-investment / exit / add-on]. Tell me who they are, what has moved in their portfolio in the last quarter, which of their deals touched ours, and every real connection between us.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Puts their portfolio moves next to yours and finds the overlaps a search would never surface. **Time saved:** 1 to 2 h.

## What did we talk about last time

**For:** Everyone. **When:** A name in the calendar you half remember.

```text wrap
@[person] is on my calendar. What did we last talk about, what did I promise, and what has changed for them since?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Reads your own emails, messages and notes for the history, then checks their side for what moved. **Time saved:** 20 min.

## Prep for a customer call

**For:** Founder, Sales. **When:** A prospect or design partner, and you want the room, not just the company.

```text wrap
I have a call with @[company] about [what]. Who is on their side, who decides, who in my network knows them, and what has the company said publicly about [topic] this year?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Buyer map plus your paths in, in one pass. **Time saved:** 45 min.

## Draft the follow-up

**For:** Everyone. **When:** The meeting just ended.

```text wrap
I just met @[person]. Based on my notes and our history, draft a short follow-up in my tone that confirms [what we agreed] and proposes [next step].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Writes in your voice from your own tone note, with the specifics of this relationship. **Time saved:** 15 min.
