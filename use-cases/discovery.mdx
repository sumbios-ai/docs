---

Copy any prompt below and paste it into Ask O. Replace the words in square brackets with your own. Type `@` before the name of a person or a company so O finds them in your network.

## Find the right people in my network

**For:** Sales. **When:** You need a short list of people from your own connections who match a description.

```text wrap
Find people in my network with more than [3,000] LinkedIn connections whose role is about [relationship intelligence]: executives, managing directors, partners. Give me the top [20].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Sorts through your own network in seconds, using details LinkedIn does not let you search by. **Saves you:** 15 to 25 min.

## Who in my network is relevant to what I am doing now

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You're starting a new project, entering a new market, or facing a new problem.

```text wrap
I'm working on [what]. Who in my network is most relevant to it, and why? Think expertise, position, and who they know. Give me the top [10] with a reason for each.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Looks at the full picture of each person rather than their job title, so the right person shows up even when their profile headline does not say it. **Saves you:** 30 to 60 min.

## Who left, and where did they go

**For:** Investor, HR. **When:** You're keeping track of the people who leave a firm or company.

```text wrap
Track senior movement in and out of @[company] over the last 18 months. Who left, where did they go, and which of them is now reachable through my network?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Shows where former employees went, from public information, and which of them you can reach. **Saves you:** 1 to 2 h.

## Find candidates in my network for a role

**For:** Founder, HR. **When:** You have a role to fill and want to look before it goes to a recruiter.

```text wrap
I'm hiring a [role]. Who in my network fits, or is one introduction away from someone who does? Give me the top [10] with why, and who could make the intro.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Includes people one introduction away, and names who can introduce you to each candidate. **Saves you:** 2 h.

## Find design partners or first customers

**For:** Founder, Sales. **When:** You have a product that needs its first ten conversations.

```text wrap
Who in my network runs or influences [function] at companies with [criteria]? I'm looking for design partners for [product]. Top [15], with the angle for each.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Matches people on their role, the kind of company they work at, and how you know them. **Saves you:** 2 h.

## Investors in a place, at a stage

**For:** Founder. **When:** You're raising money locally, or travelling.

```text wrap
Show me investors in [city] in my network who write [pre-seed / seed] cheques and are active this year. Top [10], with how I know them.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Picks investors who are actually active, not just anyone with the word investor in their headline. **Saves you:** 1 h.

## Who do I know at this company

**For:** Everyone. **When:** The simplest question, asked often.

```text wrap
Who do I know at @[company], and who there previously worked at @[other company]?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Who is there now and where they worked before, in one answer, with the overlap you asked for. **Saves you:** 10 min.

## Experts on a topic in a place

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You need someone who really knows the subject.

```text wrap
Who are the most senior people in my network working on [topic] in [place]? Rank by depth of expertise and how well I know them.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Judges how deep someone's knowledge goes from their career and their work, not from keywords. **Saves you:** 45 min.

## The most seasoned people in my network

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You want the people who have done it more than once.

```text wrap
Who are the most seasoned [entrepreneurs / operators] in my network: people who have built more than one company that ran for at least three years?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Counts what people have actually done across their whole career. **Saves you:** 1 h.
