Each prompt is ready to paste into Ask O. Swap the bracketed parts for your own, and mention people and companies with `@` so O finds them in your network.

## Find the right people in my network

**For:** Sales. **When:** You need a shortlist of people who fit a profile, from your own connections.

```text wrap
Find people in my network with more than [3,000] LinkedIn connections whose role is about [relationship intelligence]: executives, managing directors, partners. Give me the top [20].
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Filters your own network on criteria LinkedIn does not expose, in seconds. **Time saved:** 15 to 25 min.

## Who in my network is relevant to what I am doing now

**For:** Everyone. **When:** A new project, a new market, a new problem.

```text wrap
I'm working on [what]. Who in my network is most relevant to it, and why? Think expertise, position, and who they know. Give me the top [10] with a reason for each.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Matches on context rather than job title, so the right person surfaces even when their headline does not say it. **Time saved:** 30 to 60 min.

## Who left, and where did they go

**For:** Investor, HR. **When:** Tracking talent out of a firm or company.

```text wrap
Track senior movement in and out of @[company] over the last 18 months. Who left, where did they go, and which of them is now reachable through my network?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Alumni movement across the public graph, intersected with your reach. **Time saved:** 1 to 2 h.

## Find candidates in my network for a role

**For:** Founder, HR. **When:** A role to fill before it goes to a recruiter.

```text wrap
I'm hiring a [role]. Who in my network fits, or is one introduction away from someone who does? Give me the top [10] with why, and who could make the intro.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Second-degree reach with a named connector for each candidate. **Time saved:** 2 h.

## Find design partners or first customers

**For:** Founder, Sales. **When:** A product that needs its first ten conversations.

```text wrap
Who in my network runs or influences [function] at companies with [criteria]? I'm looking for design partners for [product]. Top [15], with the angle for each.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Matches on role, company profile and how you know them. **Time saved:** 2 h.

## Investors in a place, at a stage

**For:** Founder. **When:** Raising locally, or travelling.

```text wrap
Show me investors in [city] in my network who write [pre-seed / seed] cheques and are active this year. Top [10], with how I know them.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Filters on activity, not just the word investor in a headline. **Time saved:** 1 h.

## Who do I know at this company

**For:** Everyone. **When:** The simplest question, asked often.

```text wrap
Who do I know at @[company], and who there previously worked at @[other company]?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Current and past, in one answer, with the overlap you asked for. **Time saved:** 10 min.

## Experts on a topic in a place

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You need someone who actually knows.

```text wrap
Who are the most senior people in my network working on [topic] in [place]? Rank by depth of expertise and how well I know them.
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Depth from careers and output, not from keywords. **Time saved:** 45 min.

## The most seasoned people in my network

**For:** Everyone. **When:** You want the people who have done it more than once.

```text wrap
Who are the most seasoned [entrepreneurs / operators] in my network: people who have built more than one company that ran for at least three years?
```

**What O does that a search can't:** Counts what people have actually done across their whole career. **Time saved:** 1 h.
