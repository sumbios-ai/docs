O remembers the people you know, what you share with them and what happened last time, so you walk into every conversation prepared. You ask; O answers from your own network and shows its sources. It can also keep an eye on the topics you choose and tell you when something changes. It never sends anything in your name. You decide what to do next.

## How O is built

O is a network relationship memory (NRM): one place where the people you know, your conversations and your notes about them come together, so nothing you learned about someone is lost between apps.
