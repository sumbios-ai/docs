Open a profile to see someone's work, education and connections, and where each of those details comes from.

## Where to find it

Open **Network** and use the search box and filters above the map or the list.

<Frame caption="Network → List view. Other people's names and photos are blurred.">
  <img src="/images/network-and-circles/network-list.png" alt="The Network page in List view: search, Filter, Export, and a table of contacts with their company and when you were last in contact" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Search by name, or by any detail the filters on screen let you choose.
- Sort the list with the sort menu.
- Select a person in the list, or a dot on the map, to open their profile.
- Use Contact Import when you need to add contact details of a kind O supports.

## A member's profile

Select a person and their profile opens beside the page. It shows who they are, what O knows about them and what you can do next, such as adding them to a Circle or setting a reminder. [A member's profile](/network-and-circles/member-profile) explains every tab and button.

## How to use it

- If a search gives you too many results, try a less common name or organization.
- Before you change anything or contact someone, check it's the right person and look at where the details come from.
- Clear your search and filters to see everyone in the networks you chose again.

<Note>
**Good to know.** What you find depends on what O knows and which networks you chose. Two people can have similar names, so check the profile before you change anything or contact them.
</Note>
