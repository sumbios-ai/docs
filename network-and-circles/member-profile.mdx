A profile brings together everything O knows about one person: who they are, why they may matter to you, how you are connected and what has changed. The buttons down its left side let you act on that person without leaving the page.

## When you would use it

Before you contact someone, to see how you know them and what to talk about. After you meet them, to write down what you learned, set a follow-up or add them to the Circle they belong in. Or whenever you want their email, phone or birthday in one place.

## Where to find it

Select a person anywhere in O: in search, in a list on Network, in a Circle or in an answer from Ask O. Their profile opens in a panel beside the page. Pin it back to the dock or close it from the top right.

At the top: their photo, name, role and company, how you are connected (for example **1st degree**), and when they joined their current company.

| # | Element | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Save to circle** | Adds them to one or more [Circles](/network-and-circles/circles), or to a new one. |
| 2 | **Notes** | Opens their notes, where you can write a **New Note**. |
| 3 | **Set reminder** | Sets a follow-up, a meeting, a birthday or another reminder about them. |
| 4 | **Contact info** | Their email, phone, website, birthday, social handles and any extra fields you keep. |
| 5 | **LinkedIn** | Opens their LinkedIn profile in a new tab. |
| 6 | **Sync history** | Syncs their history with you. |
| 7 | **Refresh intelligence** | Updates what O has worked out about them, such as the perspectives and insights. |
| 8 | **Ask O** | Asks O about this person. |
| 9 | **Delete** | Deletes this member. Use it with care. |
| 10 | Skills | Their skills. Open the list to see all of them. |
| 11 | Tabs | **Overview**, **Insights**, **Between you**, **Recent** and **Background**, each explained below. |
| 12 | **Perspective** | The lens you look at them through: **VC**, **Hiring**, **Sales**, **Partnership**, **Ecosystem**, **Engineering**, **Board**, **Founder** and more. |
| 13 | **Why they matter for** your lens | O's short summary of why this person matters from that angle. The sparkle marks text O generated. |

You may also see an **Explore** tab. It is not switched on yet.

## How to

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add them to a Circle">
    Choose **Save to circle**. Pick one or more Circles, or choose **New Circle**, then confirm with the **Add to** button, which counts the Circles you picked. Adding someone never removes anyone already in a Circle.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/profiles/pf-circle.png" alt="Add to Circle: find a Circle, New Circle, and a list of your Circles to choose from" width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a reminder">
    Choose **Set reminder**. Pick a type (**Follow-up**, **Meeting**, **Birthday** or **Other**), write the reminder, and choose the date and time. Make it **Recurring** if it repeats, add notes if you like, then **Create Reminder**. It appears in [Notifications → Reminders](/notifications-and-conversations/reminders).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/profiles/pf-reminder.png" alt="Set a reminder: type, reminder text, related person or company, date and time, recurring, notes, and Create Reminder" width="440" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write down what you know">
    Choose **Notes**, then **New Note**. Capture a conversation, a follow-up or an insight, and it stays with this person.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/profiles/pf-notes.png" alt="Notes for a person, with no notes yet and a New Note button" width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contact details">
    Choose **Contact info**. On **Contact**, add an email, phone, website and birthday; on **Social Handles**, their X / Twitter, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky or Reddit. On **Extra fields**, define named sets of fields you want to keep for many people, for example investor fields, and use one as your default. Then **Save**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/profiles/pf-contact.png" alt="Contact and Social Info: Contact, Social Handles and Extra fields tabs, with email, phone, website and birthday" width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask about them">
    Choose **Ask O** on the left, or **Find warm intro** on Overview, to go further with O.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What O does behind it

- **Hypotheses, not facts.** O's cards are marked **Hypothesis, to verify**, and Insights ends with **What to verify**. Check them before you rely on them.
- **Evidence first.** Connection Strength and Your history come from your notes, your reminders and your connected sources. Where O has little to go on, it says so; it is a snapshot, not a verdict.
- **What you add is yours.** Your notes, reminders and contact details are kept in your account. Notes are shared with a friend only if you turn on **Notes** in their [access settings](/network-and-circles/friends-and-sharing).
- **O never contacts them.** Nothing on a profile sends a message or reaches out in your name.

<Note>
**About other people.** A profile shows what O has gathered about someone from your sources and public information. Use it to prepare and to remember, and treat what you read about people with care.
</Note>
