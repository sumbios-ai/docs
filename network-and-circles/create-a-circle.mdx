description: "Give your Circle a name that helps you recognize it."
---

Depending on the options shown, you can add a description, choose members, and decide how private it should be.

## Where to find it

From Circles, choose Create or the owned circle’s Edit action.

## What you can do

- Enter or revise the name and description.
- Choose the type and add tags when those fields are present.
- Select members and privacy options offered by the form.
- Save to create or update the circle; cancel to leave it unchanged.

## How to use it

- Use a name that describes your own organizing purpose.
- Check selected members and privacy before saving.
- Reopen the circle to confirm the saved member count and details.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Only manage information you have permission to use. Available fields can differ by account and ownership; shared circles may be read-only.
</Note>
