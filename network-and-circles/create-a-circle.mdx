title: "Create or edit a Circle"
description: "Give your Circle a name you'll recognize."
---

Depending on what the form shows, you can also add a description, choose who is in it and decide how private it is.

## Where to find it

In **Circles**, choose Create for a new Circle, or Edit on a Circle you own.

## What you can do

- Type or change the name and description.
- Choose a type and add tags, if the form has those fields.
- Choose members and any privacy options the form offers.
- Save to create or update the Circle. Cancel to leave it as it was.

## How to use it

- Pick a name that tells you what you're using the Circle for.
- Check the members and privacy before you save.
- Open the Circle again to check that the number of members and the details were saved.

<Note>
**Good to know.** Only add or change information you have permission to use. The fields you see can depend on your account and on whether you own the Circle. A Circle someone shared with you may be view only.
</Note>
