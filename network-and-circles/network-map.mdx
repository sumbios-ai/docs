title: "Explore your Network map"
description: "See who you know and how the people you know are connected to each other."
---

The map shows your network as a picture. Switch to the list when you want to find and look at one person at a time.

## Where to find it

Select **Network** in the sidebar. The buttons at the top of the page switch between the map and the list and change the view.

## What you can do

- Switch between the map and a list of people with the view buttons.
- Search for a person, or for something else such as a company.
- Choose whose network to see: yours, the networks friends share with you, or both. Then pick the friends you want to include.
- Select a person or item to see more about it. When the import and summary options appear, you can use them too.
- Use the map's view, filter and layout options to focus on the connections you care about.

## How to use it

- Start by choosing which networks to include. Adding or removing a friend's network changes the connections you see.
- Select a dot on the map to open the person or item it stands for.
- If you can't find someone, clear your search and filters.
- The list is better for finding one exact person. The map is better for looking around.

<Note>
**Good to know.** The map only shows connections O has information about. Where a person sits on the map, the lines between people and their colors don't tell you how much people trust each other or whether they are willing to help.
</Note>
