---
title: "Open and manage a Circle"
description: "Explore the members and updates in this Circle."
---

Overview gives you the summary, People lists its members, Map shows their connections, and Signals brings together available updates. Some Circles also show News and Activity.

## Where to find it

Select a circle card or its Open menu action.

## What you can do

- Choose Overview for the circle summary or People to browse its members.
- Choose Map to explore connections or Signals to catch up on available updates.
- Use Feed settings to choose the sections you see. If News and Activity tabs are shown, switch between them to browse updates.
- Open the circle menu to edit, export, delete, or start a chat where those actions are offered.
- Select a member to open their profile.

## How to use it

- Confirm you opened the intended Circle before changing who is in it.
- Use the owner controls for edits; contact the owner if a shared circle needs a change.
- Return to Circles to switch to another group.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** A Circle is your way of organizing members; it does not mean they have joined a group together. It stays private unless you share it or the page says it is shared.
</Note>
