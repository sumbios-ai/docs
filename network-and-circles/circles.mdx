Browse your Circles, see who is in each one, and check which ones have been shared with you.

## Where to find it

Select Circles in the sidebar.

<Frame caption="Circles">
  <img src="/images/network-and-circles/circles.png" alt="The Circles page with Create Circle, search and filters, and one private Circle with two members" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Choose New/Create to start a Circle for members you want to keep together.
- Choose a Circle to explore its members, or select Open from its menu.
- Use Edit to change a circle you own.
- Use Delete to remove a circle you own after reviewing the confirmation.
- Open the circle’s Chat action where available.

## How to use it

- Use the search/filter controls to narrow the circle list.
- Look for the private and shared labels so you know who can see the Circle.
- Use the card menu rather than selecting the card when you intend to edit or delete.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Shared circles are not editable by recipients from the owner-only menu. Deletion is destructive; confirm the circle name and contents first.
</Note>
