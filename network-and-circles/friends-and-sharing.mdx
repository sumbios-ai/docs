You stay in control of which network information you share and with whom.

## Where to find it

Open Network → Friends at /network/friends or select shared-network controls from Network.

## What you can do

- See who you are connected with and which networks are being shared.
- Use the sharing switches to choose what you share with each friend.
- On the Network page, include a friend’s network when you want to explore the connections they have shared.
- Open or respond to a sharing invitation using the action shown.

## How to use it

- Review each sharing setting before changing it.
- Choose only the friends whose networks you intend to include in a view.
- Turn off or limit sharing when you no longer want to share that information with someone.

<Note>
**Availability and privacy.** Shared network access is governed by the sharing permissions shown. Shared data belongs to the person who provided it; do not redistribute it without permission.
</Note>
